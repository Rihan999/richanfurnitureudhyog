WHO WE ARE

Creating Furniture That Lasts for Generations

Richan Furniture Udhyog is a furniture manufacturer based in Bhaktapur, Nepal. We specialize in premium wardrobes, beds, sofas, dining tables, office furniture, and custom-made solutions designed to match every customer's needs.

Every piece is carefully built by experienced craftsmen using quality materials and modern manufacturing techniques. Our goal is simple—deliver furniture that combines durability, comfort, and timeless design.

Whether you're furnishing a single room or an entire home, our team is committed to providing personalized service, honest pricing, and excellent workmanship.
